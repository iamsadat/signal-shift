Signal Shift

Tech and AI news, minus the hype.

Signal Shift is an animated YouTube channel. Our host, a skeptical 1950s TV-set robot, reads the fine print on new gadgets and AI products and gives an honest verdict, with every claim checked against its source.

Signal Shift Autopilot is the private tool the channel uses to upload its own videos to its own YouTube channel and to read that channel's analytics. It is used only by the channel owner.
